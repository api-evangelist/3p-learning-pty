If you’ve ever listened to a student read aloud and thought, “They know the words, but they don’t really understand what they just read,” you’re not alone. Many students can decode accurately, yet comprehension still feels just out of reach. The missing piece is often fluency – not speed, but the ability to read accurately, automatically, and with expression so meaning can take center stage.
