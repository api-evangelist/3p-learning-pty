# 3P Learning

> 3P Learning is an educational technology company that builds blended learning software for mathematics and literacy — including Mathletics, Mathseeds, Reading Eggs, Writing Legends, LiteracyPlanet and Brightpath — used by more than 20 million students across 130+ countries. It is backed by Insight Partners and profiled in the API Evangelist network. 3P Learning publishes consumer and school-facing web and mobile products but does not currently expose a public first-party developer API.

## Products
- [Mathletics](https://www.3plearning.com/software/mathletics/): Engaging mathematics program for school and home.
- [Mathseeds](https://www.3plearning.com/software/mathseeds/): Early-years mathematics learning.
- [Reading Eggs](https://www.3plearning.com/software/reading-eggs/): Learn-to-read program for young children.
- [Writing Legends](https://www.3plearning.com/software/writing-legends/): Writing practice and feedback.
- [LiteracyPlanet](https://www.3plearning.com/software/literacyplanet/): Comprehensive literacy program.
- [Brightpath](https://www.3plearning.com/software/brightpath/): Formative writing assessment.
